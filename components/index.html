Dataset, endpoint & limitations

This model was refitted on 232 eligible adult eye pairs from 130 patients. The 426 historical inventory eyes include missing or ineligible follow-up records. The fit uses first-visit age and measurements; later measurements define the outcome, never the predictors.

Validation kept both eyes of a patient together. The model was developed on variable follow-up from 90 days to five years and is evaluated here at one year. The 9–15-month subset is only a descriptive internal audit, not fixed-horizon external validation.

The selected CXL/control sample and incomplete treatment/quality audit limit clinical interpretation. The percentage is a working research-model response to the entered values; it must not be used to diagnose progression, schedule follow-up or decide CXL. It differs from the longitudinal reference-evidence assessment below.
